Police and immediate danger

Is it an emergency?

Call Triple Zero (000)

Use 000 when someone is seriously injured, a life or property is being threatened, or a crime is happening now and police, fire or ambulance attendance is urgent.

Non-urgent police

Use Policelink online or call 131 444 when police do not need to attend immediately.

Hooning or traffic complaint

Use the QPS reporting form or call 134 666. If danger is immediate, call 000.

Lost property

Use Queensland Police’s lost-property option through Policelink rather than posting identifying details publicly.

Suspicious activity

Use the relevant Policelink pathway. Do not confront someone or publish an accusation in a community group.

Local infrastructure and public places

Report a Council issue

Fraser Coast Regional Council’s request portal accepts reports about trees, paths, graffiti, dumped rubbish, bins, water faults, park maintenance, barking dogs and other Council services.

The portal sends the issue and location to the relevant team and provides a reference number. You can also call Council on 1300 79 49 29.

Domestic animals and wildlife

Lost pet, wandering animal or injured wildlife

Lost pet

Check Council’s live impounded-animal list and contact the Animal Facility on 1300 79 49 29. Proof of ownership and photo identification are required to reclaim an animal.

Found dog or cat

Council accepts found domestic animals at the Fraser Coast Adoption Centre and Pound in Cicada Lane, Urangan, during its published opening hours. A vet can check for a microchip.

Ranger pickup

Council lists weekday pickup hours and requires someone to be present. Call 1300 79 49 29 for the current arrangement.

Injured or neglected animal

Call RSPCA Queensland on 1300 264 625 (1300 ANIMAL). Avoid handling wildlife unless the service advises you to do so.

Read Council’s current lost-and-found process

Electrical safety and outages

Power outage or fallen line

  • Life-threatening electrical emergency: call 000 or Ergon Energy on 13 16 70.
  • Lost power or supply problem: check Ergon’s Outage Finder or call 13 22 96.
  • Stay well clear of fallen powerlines and anything touching them. Treat every line as live.

Open Ergon Energy’s outage and safety page

On the water

Marine emergency, assistance and trip logging

In a marine emergency, use VHF channel 16 or call 000. Marine Rescue Queensland lists 131 MRQ (131 677) as its phone contact and VHF channel 73 for Hervey Bay log on and log off.

MRQ Hervey Bay publishes current radio hours, channels and unit details on its official page. Check them before departure rather than relying on an old saved number.

Check MRQ Hervey Bay

Useful evidence, safer reporting

Report facts without creating a public incident file

  • Record the time, place and what you directly observed.
  • Give identifying details, photographs or video to the responsible authority through its secure process.
  • Do not publish home addresses, phone numbers, number plates, medical information or unverified accusations on Facebook.
  • Community-post volume is not a crime-rate measure and should not be used to label Point Vernon unsafe.
