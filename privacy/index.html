At a glance

What this site collects

Point Vernon Guide does not ask visitors to create an account, post comments, submit a web form or make a payment. It uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how the public guide is found and used.

The web host may still process standard technical information needed to deliver and protect the site under its own terms and privacy practices.

Anonymous usage trends

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 records aggregated information such as page views, scrolls, outbound-link clicks, approximate location, device and browser type, and the website or campaign that referred a visit. The guide uses this information to improve its pages and navigation. It does not use Analytics to sell advertising or create user accounts.

Google processes Analytics data under its own terms and privacy policy. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses, but it may use an IP address temporarily to derive approximate location before discarding it.

Browser storage

Cookies and your choices

Google Analytics may use first-party cookies, including identifiers beginning with _ga, to distinguish visits and calculate usage statistics. External websites reached through this guide may set their own cookies or use browser storage under their own policies.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser, block the Google Analytics script, or use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on. These choices should not prevent the static Point Vernon Guide pages from loading.

Other websites

External links

This guide links to public agencies, community organisations, mapping or transport services, visitor information and business directories. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Opening an external link may allow that provider to receive information such as your IP address, browser details and the page that referred you.

Public repository

Correction requests

The guide links to a public GitHub issue form for corrections and event suggestions. Information submitted there may be publicly visible with the contributor’s GitHub account details. Do not include private or sensitive personal information.

GitHub handles that service under its own privacy practices. A correction can also be supported by a public agency or organiser link without naming a private individual.

Keeping this accurate

Policy updates and questions

This privacy information may be updated when the site’s tools or practices change. Material changes should be reflected here before a new service is enabled.
