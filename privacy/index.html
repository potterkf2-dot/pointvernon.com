At a glance

What this site collects

Point Vernon Guide does not ask visitors to create an account, post comments, submit a web form or make a payment. Google Analytics 4 is off by default and its script is not requested unless a visitor chooses “Allow Analytics”.

GitHub Pages hosts the website, and Cloudflare helps deliver and protect it. Those services may process standard technical information needed to respond to a request and maintain security under their own privacy practices.

Usage trends

Analytics

Allowing Analytics helps the publisher understand which guides are useful and whether visitors can find maps, services and official information. You can read and use the whole website without allowing it.

If a visitor allows Analytics, the site grants Analytics storage and the Google tag may set first-party Analytics cookies, such as _ga, to distinguish visits and measure use of the guide. Advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation remain denied. Google Signals and advertising personalisation stay disabled, and the guide does not use Analytics to create advertising profiles or user accounts.

The measured activity includes page views and Google Analytics 4’s standard session and engagement events. Enhanced Measurement may also record scrolls, outbound-link clicks, file downloads, on-site search results, embedded-video interactions, and form starts or submissions when those features are present. The guide also sends selected navigation actions: homepage pathway and guide choices, clicks to official sources, map links, and phone or email links. Event data may include the current page, a general guide or pathway name, link position, destination domain, source category, map provider, or a non-personal service label. The site does not send the phone number, email address, message contents, property address or a visitor-entered value as an Analytics event parameter.

Google processes Analytics data under its own terms and privacy policy. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses, but it may use an IP address temporarily to derive approximate location before discarding it.

Your choice

Browser storage and your choices

Your choiceWhat happens on this site
No choice yetAnalytics stays off. The Google Analytics script is not loaded.
Allow AnalyticsAnalytics can measure visits and selected actions, using first-party Analytics cookies. Advertising features remain disabled.
No thanksThe site stops Analytics measurement and does not load its script on subsequent pages while that choice is saved.

You can also use “Privacy settings” in any page’s footer. Changing your choice applies to this browser, including other open Point Vernon Guide tabs that share its local storage. It does not change the choice in a different browser or on another device.

The site saves “allow” or “decline” in local storage. If storage is blocked, permission applies only to the current page and the next page asks again. If the saved choice is cleared, Analytics stays off until permission is given again. Previously set Analytics cookies may remain until they expire or you remove them in browser settings; declining stops further measurement by this site but does not delete previously collected data.

Technical details and earlier choices

The current local-storage key is point-vernon-analytics-choice-v2. Earlier permission for cookieless measurement is not reused as permission for Analytics cookies, so those visitors are asked again. An earlier “No thanks” choice is retained.

Other websites

External links

This guide links to public agencies, community organisations, mapping or transport services, visitor information and business directories. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Opening an external link may allow that provider to receive information such as your IP address, browser details and the page that referred you.

Email contact

Correction requests

The guide accepts corrections and event suggestions by email. A message contains the address and information the sender chooses to provide, and is handled by the email providers involved in its delivery. Do not include private or sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

A correction can be supported by a public agency or organiser link without naming a private individual.

Keeping this accurate

Policy updates and questions

This privacy information may be updated when the site’s tools or practices change. Material changes should be reflected here before a new service is enabled.

For a privacy question or correction, email hello@pointvernon.com.
