At a glance

What this site collects

Point Vernon Guide does not ask visitors to create an account, post comments, submit a web form or make a payment. It uses Google Analytics 4 in a privacy-limited mode to understand broad trends in how the public guide is found and used. Analytics, advertising and personalisation storage are denied before the Google tag loads.

The web host may still process standard technical information needed to deliver and protect the site under its own terms and privacy practices.

Anonymous usage trends

Analytics

With Analytics storage denied, the Google tag can send limited, cookieless measurements rather than reading or writing Analytics cookies. These measurements may include basic events and page information. The guide uses resulting aggregate trends to improve pages and navigation; it disables Google advertising signals and does not use Analytics to sell advertising or create user accounts.

Google processes Analytics data under its own terms and privacy policy. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses, but it may use an IP address temporarily to derive approximate location before discarding it.

Browser storage

Browser storage and your choices

This site sets Analytics and advertising storage to denied and does not provide a control that changes that setting. The Google tag should therefore use cookieless measurement rather than first-party Analytics identifiers such as _ga. External websites reached through this guide may set their own cookies or use browser storage under their own policies.

You can also block the Google Analytics script with browser settings or content-blocking software. That choice should not prevent the static Point Vernon Guide pages from loading.

Other websites

External links

This guide links to public agencies, community organisations, mapping or transport services, visitor information and business directories. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Opening an external link may allow that provider to receive information such as your IP address, browser details and the page that referred you.

Email contact

Correction requests

The guide accepts corrections and event suggestions by email. A message contains the address and information the sender chooses to provide, and is handled by the email providers involved in its delivery. Do not include private or sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

A correction can be supported by a public agency or organiser link without naming a private individual.

Keeping this accurate

Policy updates and questions

This privacy information may be updated when the site’s tools or practices change. Material changes should be reflected here before a new service is enabled.

For a privacy question or correction, email hello@pointvernon.com.
