Privacy

Last updated: 22/08/2026

This site is designed to collect very little information. It has no visitor account system, advertising profile, comments or payment form.

At a glance

What this site collects

Point Vernon Guide does not ask visitors to create an account, post comments or make a payment. The optional newsletter form asks only for an email address. Google Analytics 4 is off by default and its script is not requested unless a visitor chooses “Allow anonymous Analytics”.

The web host may still process standard technical information needed to deliver and protect the site under its own terms and privacy practices.

Anonymous usage trends

Analytics

If a visitor allows Analytics, advertising, personalisation and Analytics storage remain denied. The Google tag can then send limited, cookieless measurements rather than reading or writing Analytics cookies. The guide uses aggregate trends to improve pages and navigation; it disables Google advertising signals and does not use Analytics to create advertising profiles or user accounts.

The measured actions are page views and selected navigation actions: homepage pathway and guide choices, clicks to official sources, map links, and phone or email links. Event data may include the current page, a general guide or pathway name, link position, destination domain, source category, map provider, or a non-personal service label. The site does not send the phone number, email address, message contents, property address or a visitor-entered value as an Analytics event parameter.

Google processes Analytics data under its own terms and privacy policy. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses, but it may use an IP address temporarily to derive approximate location before discarding it.

Browser storage

Browser storage and your choices

The site stores the choice “allow” or “decline” in the browser’s local storage under point-vernon-analytics-choice. This prevents the question being repeated on every page. Choosing “No thanks” means the Google Analytics script is not loaded on later page views.

Use the “Privacy settings” button in the footer to change the choice. If browser storage is blocked or cleared, Analytics remains off until permission is given again. External websites reached through this guide may set their own cookies or use browser storage under their own policies.

Optional email updates

Newsletter subscriptions

When a visitor submits the newsletter form, the email address, IP address and referrer-level information are sent directly to Buttondown. Buttondown manages the subscriber record, confirmation, delivery, unsubscribes and invalid-address cleanup for Point Vernon Guide.

Every subscription uses double opt-in: the address is not added as a confirmed subscriber until its owner clicks the confirmation link. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. Point Vernon Guide does not sell or rent the subscriber list.

Buttondown’s optional open, click, transactional and web tracking are disabled for this newsletter. The form does not load a Buttondown script or place a Buttondown cookie on Point Vernon Guide before it is submitted. Read Buttondown’s privacy policy.

Other websites

External links

This guide links to public agencies, community organisations, mapping or transport services, visitor information and business directories. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Opening an external link may allow that provider to receive information such as your IP address, browser details and the page that referred you.

Email contact

Correction requests

The guide accepts corrections and event suggestions by email. A message contains the address and information the sender chooses to provide, and is handled by the email providers involved in its delivery. Do not include private or sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

A correction can be supported by a public agency or organiser link without naming a private individual.

Keeping this accurate

Policy updates and questions

This privacy information may be updated when the site’s tools or practices change. Material changes should be reflected here before a new service is enabled.

For a privacy question or correction, email hello@pointvernon.com.
