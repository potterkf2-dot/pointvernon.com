Match the stop to the plan

Choose food for your outing

  • Coffee before a wander: contact Brew & View first, then choose your foreshore starting point. A cafe’s closing time may differ from its kitchen’s last orders.
  • Takeaway for the park: confirm Point Vernon Takeaway’s collection time, then head to your chosen picnic spot. Bring drinks, napkins and whatever your order needs.
  • A barbecue lunch: check Fraser Coast Gourmet Meats for supplies, and check the park’s barbecue facilities before shopping. Have a ready-to-eat alternative if the barbecue is occupied or unavailable.
  • A few forgotten supplies: the 5 Ways listing is a starting point for checking stock. For a full grocery shop or a particular dietary item, confirm availability before making a detour.

Planning an evening meal? Confirm a local order before leaving your accommodation or broaden the search to central Hervey Bay. These listings do not establish late-night trading or delivery.

Take it outside

Plan a foreshore picnic

Choose the park before collecting food. ESA Park combines listed picnic facilities with a playground and toilets. The Gables has a shelter, seating, barbecue and water tap, but Council does not list a toilet there. These are separate destinations; pick the facilities your group needs. For a shoreline stop, use the Gatakers Bay guide to plan around tides and the working ramp.

  • Carry drinking water and take every item of rubbish away if bins are full.
  • Use the barbecue only when it is available, clean and not affected by a local restriction.
  • Keep food away from wildlife and do not feed birds.
  • Leave boat-ramp approaches and shared paths clear.
