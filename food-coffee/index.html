Small and local

Three useful neighbourhood stops

These are practical starting points, not rankings or endorsements. Small businesses can change names, menus and hours quickly, so use the linked listing as a lead and confirm directly before making a special trip.

Brew & View

A neighbourhood cafe listing on the Esplanade, suited to coffee or a simple daytime stop. Older online references may use a previous business name, which is one reason current details matter.

Point Vernon Takeaway

A local takeaway listed at Preston Street. Confirm its current menu, dietary options, ordering method and collection times directly.

5 Ways Convenience

A Corser Street convenience-store listing that may be useful for drinks, snacks and basic supplies. Product range and trading hours can vary.

Confirm directly before travelling

Business names, trading status, opening hours, menus, accessibility and dietary information can change. The links below are starting points rather than guarantees.

Take it outside

Plan a foreshore picnic

Takeaway is especially useful here because the headland has several places to pause near the water. ESA Park and The Gables have Council-listed picnic facilities, while Gatakers Bay offers a calmer western outlook. Choose a spot that suits the wind, tide and mobility needs of your group.

  • Carry drinking water and take every item of rubbish away if bins are full.
  • Use the barbecue only when it is available, clean and not affected by a local restriction.
  • Keep food away from wildlife and do not feed birds.
  • Leave boat-ramp approaches and shared paths clear.
