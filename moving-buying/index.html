Schools, childcare and electricity setup

Community noticeboards contain many recommendations, but catchments, approved care services, vacancies and electricity arrangements need current official checks.

State school catchments

Use Queensland Government’s EdMap with the exact residential address, enrolment year and year level. Catchment boundaries are reviewed and can change; contact the school about enrolment and evidence requirements.

Search the official EdMap

Approved childcare

StartingBlocks publishes approved-service details and regulatory quality ratings. It currently lists Goodstart Early Learning Point Vernon as a centre-based long-day-care service. Vacancy and fee information can change, so confirm directly.

Check the StartingBlocks service record

Opening an electricity account

Ergon Energy Network is the local electricity distributor, not the service that handles ordinary billing or a new retail account. Ergon Energy Retail has a moving-home process for regional Queensland. Confirm the arrangement for the exact premises, especially in an embedded network or caravan park.

On-ground and professional checks

Questions this website cannot settle

Inspect the property and the routine

Noise, drainage, mobile reception, shade, traffic, path continuity, accessibility, street parking and the condition of a particular building require current evidence. Visit at relevant times and use qualified professionals where the decision warrants it.

  • Confirm planning and hazard information for the exact lot.
  • Ask an insurer about the particular property before committing.
  • Test essential journeys at the times you expect to make them.
  • Check internet, power, water and other service arrangements with the responsible provider.
  • Record claims made in advertising and verify them independently.
