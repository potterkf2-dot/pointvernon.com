On-ground and professional checks

Questions this website cannot settle

Inspect the property and the routine

Noise, drainage, mobile reception, shade, traffic, path continuity, accessibility, street parking and the condition of a particular building require current evidence. Visit at relevant times and use qualified professionals where the decision warrants it.

  • Confirm planning and hazard information for the exact lot.
  • Ask an insurer about the particular property before committing.
  • Test essential journeys at the times you expect to make them.
  • Check internet, power, water and other service arrangements with the responsible provider.
  • Record claims made in advertising and verify them independently.
