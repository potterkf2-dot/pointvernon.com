Point Vernon boat ramps: Gatakers Bay and The Gables

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Choose between Gatakers Bay and The Gables using Council’s individual facility records, then check the ramp, tide and conditions for your own vessel and return trip.

  • Gatakers BayTwo lanes; Council lists no low-tide restriction
  • The GablesOne lane; low-tide restricted
  • FacilitiesNeither ramp lists water or toilets

Use the Gatakers Bay facility record or The Gables facility record for the authority’s map and current details. The local map guide also distinguishes the two locations.

A tide restriction is only part of the decision

The Gables’ low-tide restriction matters for both launching and retrieving. Plan the return as carefully as departure. Council’s “No” entry for Gatakers Bay does not guarantee enough depth for every boat, draft or weather condition.

Check the tide and marine weather sources, the current channel and any signs before committing to a launch. Council’s load-limit entry should also be read with the on-site sign; ask the facility manager if you are unsure how it applies to your setup.

Check servicing before choosing a launch time

Council services ramps near low tide where practicable. Cleaning or inspection can partly or fully close a ramp and cause delays; scheduled dates may change.

Open the “Scheduled Cleaning” section of the individual ramp record before each trip. Use Council’s boating-facility directory to compare an alternative if work affects your planned launch. Contact Council on 1300 79 49 29 when the current arrangement is unclear.

A published cleaning date is a planned service, not evidence of an all-day closure. Follow signs and staff directions on arrival.

Plan water, toilets and shore-side waiting

Both ramp records list no water or toilets. Bring drinking water and arrange any toilet stop separately. A water tap listed at The Gables park does not establish that a boat-ramp wash-down facility is available.

For listed toilets and picnic facilities, compare ESA Park as a separate stop. Keep people waiting ashore out of reversing areas, leave trailer manoeuvring space clear and do not treat a launching lane as beach access.

Once you leave the ramp

Check the exact zone and designated areas on the Great Sandy Marine Park maps. Natural reef, the offshore artificial reef and the Gatakers Bay and Gables Point no-anchoring areas are different features. Use the fishing guide for the rules checklist.

Marine Rescue Queensland Hervey Bay lists VHF channel 73 for logging on and off, with radio hours of 6 am–6 pm daily. Check current arrangements before departure and log off on return. In an emergency, use VHF channel 16 or call Triple Zero (000).

Sources and review

Facility details were checked against Council’s two individual ramp records on 19 September 2026. No ramp or channel inspection was undertaken. Links above lead to the responsible authorities; this guide does not provide live depth, a navigation route or a launch clearance.
