How credits work

A contributed photograph is credited beside its caption or directly beneath the image. The wording is agreed with the photographer before publication.

This register gives each photograph’s file reference, the photographer’s chosen credit and the pages displaying it. Different sizes and file formats share the same reference. The aerial shoreline photograph is also used in link previews across the site.

A clear record of changes

Image editing and privacy

Website copies are cropped, resized and lightly adjusted for clear display.

  • Ground-level foreshore and playground: tightly localised, AI-assisted people removal protects privacy. Only the small areas people obscured were reconstructed; the wider setting was preserved.
  • Esplanade walking photograph: three rear-facing people with no visible faces remain in the image, and a vehicle registration is blurred.
  • Kangaroo photograph: a restrained conventional tonal adjustment only. No animal or habitat detail was generated or removed.

Photographs illustrate a place at the time they were taken. They do not confirm today’s conditions, available facilities or access.

Photographers remain in control

Permission and removal

Contributors keep their copyright and may continue to use, share or sell their own photographs. Permission given to Point Vernon Guide does not transfer ownership.

A contributor can ask for a photograph or credit wording to be changed or removed at any time. Email the file reference or page address to hello@pointvernon.com, and say which image or wording you mean. If you are pictured and have a privacy concern, you can use the same contact.
