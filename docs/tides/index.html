Point Vernon tides and weather

Last updated: 11 October 2026

The tide does more than move the waterline. It changes how much sand and rock is exposed, how far you may need to walk to the water and which routes across the shore remain sensible.

Use the Point Vernon forecast and BOM warnings and alerts before setting out.

What the coast looks like

Lower tide

More rock shelves, shallow pools and sand flats appear. The waterline moves farther from the high-tide beach.

Higher tide

Water covers the shelves and comes closer to the reserve. The strip of dry beach becomes narrower.

These beaches are unpatrolled. Check the tide before walking onto the rock flats; keep a clear route back and stay clear of boat ramps.

Launching a boat

The ramp comparison covers the two launch points and The Gables’ low-tide restriction. Use current marine charts, weather and notices for a boating trip.
