Your first hour by the bay

Start at Gatakers Bay’s Corfield Street approach. Spend a few minutes looking across the bay, take a short return walk beside the reserve, then pause with a picnic. This is an outing plan; choose your own turnaround.

For a visit centred on play and park facilities, choose ESA Park instead. Find your starting place.

Add a picnic and local history

Make The Gables a second stop and read the boarding-house story before looking at Dunn’s Enclosure’s tidal remains. Plan the transfer between destinations using your own transport.

For bushland, choose Parraweena Park beside Eli Creek. For a measured walk, use the paved 5 km foreshore return.

Where is Point Vernon?

Point Vernon is a coastal suburb at the north-western end of Hervey Bay’s urban shoreline, on Queensland’s Fraser Coast. The Esplanade follows much of the coast, connecting residential streets with parks and bay views.

Pialba is the nearby centre for shops and services. Whale-tour departures are in the Urangan harbour area; allow separately for travel to the operator’s meeting point.

Arriving and getting around

Choose a named park or street access as your destination. Park only where permitted and keep driveways and trailer manoeuvring space clear.

Without a car, use the booked on-demand service and arrange the return journey. Two local holiday parks offer a base for a longer stay.

Before heading onto the shore

These beaches are unpatrolled. Check the tide before walking onto the rock flats; keep a clear route back and stay clear of boat ramps.

Bring water, sun protection and secure footwear. Read the dog boundaries for a visit with a dog.
