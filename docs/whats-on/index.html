Saturday parkrun

Point Vernon Foreshore Reserve parkrun is a free weekly 5 km at 7:00am, starting opposite 138 The Esplanade. Walk, jog, run, volunteer or watch. Find the start and registration details.

Use the event’s official channels for cancellations and the next event.

Nearby events and activities

For library sessions, markets, festivals and public events elsewhere in Hervey Bay, browse Council’s events calendar and the Fraser Coast visitor website. Use the organiser’s page for the venue and bookings.

For water play in Pialba, open WetSide’s current opening and works information.

The bay through the year

Hervey Bay’s whale season is generally July to October. Read the whale guide for shore-side viewing and tour departures. For an unscheduled day, choose a walk, park or history stop.

Suggest a local activity

Email a public event’s organiser link, venue and booking details to hello@pointvernon.com.
