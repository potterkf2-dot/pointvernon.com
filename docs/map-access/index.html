Find a place by the bay

Tap a number on the map or choose a place below. Each stop has a short description, its guide and directions. North is at the top of the map.

  1. 1. Gatakers Bay ramp

    A working boat ramp beside the bay’s narrow beach and tidal rock shelves.

    Guide · Directions
  2. 2. ESA Park

    Playground, picnic shelters, barbecues, toilets and accessible park facilities.

    Guide · Directions
  3. 3. The Gables park

    A foreshore picnic stop with shelter, seating, barbecue, water and a local-history story.

    Guide · Directions
  4. 4. Parraweena Park

    Bushland and the former bird-sanctuary site beside Eli Creek.

    Guide · Directions
  5. 5. The Gables ramp

    The Aplin Street launch point. Use the boat-ramp guide and current signs to plan your visit.

    Guide · Directions
  6. 6. Webb Park

    Murphy Street playground, basketball, seating and shelter.

    Guide · Directions
  7. 7. Polson Cemetery

    An active cemetery and place of remembrance. Visit quietly and give mourners privacy.

    Guide · Directions

Choose your outing

Compare park facilities, follow the paved 5 km foreshore route or plan a first hour at Gatakers Bay. For dog walks, use the signed boundaries.

Point Vernon beaches are unpatrolled. Check the tide before walking onto rock flats and keep clear of working boat ramps.
