Choose your outing

Compare park facilities, follow the paved 5 km foreshore route or plan a first hour at Gatakers Bay. For dog walks, use the signed boundaries.

Point Vernon beaches are unpatrolled. Check the tide before walking onto rock flats and keep clear of working boat ramps.
