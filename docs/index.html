Point Vernon.
Life by the bay.

Point Vernon is a coastal suburb on the north-western edge of Hervey Bay, Queensland. Rocky shores, foreshore walks and picnic parks sit beside the bay, with Pialba nearby and Urangan’s harbour farther along the coast.

Before you visit

A few local answers.

Where should I start?

Choose Gatakers Bay for shoreline views or ESA Park for a picnic and playground. The Esplanade follows much of the coast; choose a named stop so you arrive at the part you want.

Find Point Vernon parks and access points →

Can I walk beside the beach?

Start with a short foreshore walk and turn back when it suits you. For a set distance, the local parkrun course is a 5 km out-and-back route on a paved shared path.

Explore the 5 km foreshore walk →

Is Point Vernon a place to swim?

Its beaches are unpatrolled, with rocky tidal areas and boating activity at the ramps. Compare the shoreline sections and review conditions before deciding whether to enter the water.
