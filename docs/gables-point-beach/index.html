The Gables foreshore

The Gables is a foreshore park on The Esplanade, beside a narrow high-tide beach and broad rock flats. The longer Gables Point shoreline continues along the southern side of Point Vernon.

A picnic and a little history

See The Gables picnic facilities and find the park. Read the boarding-house story before looking at the shoreline remains associated with Dunn’s Enclosure.

The nearby Aplin Street ramp is a separate boating destination. Compare the two ramps for launch planning.

Lower water reveals the rock

At low water, the shore opens out into linear rock flats. At higher water, less dry beach remains. View stone remains without climbing on or moving them.

These beaches are unpatrolled. Check the tide before walking onto the rock flats; keep a clear route back and stay clear of boat ramps.
