Two public launch points

Gatakers Bay is approached from Corfield Street; The Gables ramp is approached from Aplin Street. Compare lanes, facilities and tide restrictions before choosing a launch point.

The Point Vernon Artificial Reef lies offshore and is separate from the natural shoreline reef.

Marine-park zones and fishing rules

Find your exact fishing position on the Great Sandy Marine Park maps. In a conservation park zone (yellow), line fishing is limited to two lines or rods per person and two hooks or lures per line.

Gatakers Bay and Gables Point have designated no-anchoring areas. Use the zoning map and designated-area rules to locate their boundaries.

Queensland species, size, possession and closure rules also apply. Identify your catch before keeping it and take discarded line and tackle away.

Fishing beside shared public space

These beaches are unpatrolled. Check the tide before walking onto the rock flats; keep a clear route back and stay clear of boat ramps.

Choose space where hooks and line stay clear of other shore users and working boat access.
