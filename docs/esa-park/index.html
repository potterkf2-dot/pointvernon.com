ESA Park Playground, Point Vernon

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Plan an ESA Park visit on the Point Vernon Esplanade. Find the playground, picnic shelters, barbecues, toilets, parking and nearby foreshore walks.

Where is ESA Park?

ESA Park is on the Esplanade between Aplin and Flinders Streets in Point Vernon. Fraser Coast Regional Council lists off-street parking and beach access. Start with the Council park map to find the playground and car park.

Play, picnic and shore

The playground sits back from the road. Council lists picnic shelters, barbecues, seating, showers and a water tap. ESA Park is a practical stop if you want to combine playground time and a picnic before continuing along the foreshore. Bring water and plan lunch around the shelters if your group needs a place to sit.

The foreshore and tidal rock pools are beside the park. What is exposed changes with the tide. Use the park and foreshore path if you prefer firm ground. Check the local tide and weather guide before making rock pools the main part of the outing.

Toilets, access and dogs

Council lists toilets, including an all-abilities toilet. Check the park record or ask Council if your visit depends on a particular path, ramp or accessible parking bay. For dogs, use the foreshore dog guide and follow local signs, as rules vary along the coast.

Where to go next

Make ESA Park the main stop if the playground and a picnic matter most. Add Gatakers Bay for the rocky headland or stay on the Esplanade for a shorter foreshore walk. The Point Vernon parks guide compares other nearby options.

Travelling with children? See the Hervey Bay Kids playground guide for more family-specific planning details.
