Bushland on the western foreshore

Parraweena Park is a 5.8-hectare bushland reserve between Eli Creek and Eli Point. Start at the corner of Spence and Corser streets for remnant eucalypt forest and the story of a former bird sanctuary.

Surviving features include a stone footbridge, two bench seats and the remains of a wishing well. Choose a short bushland visit and leave natural and built features undisturbed.

The Cook family’s bird sanctuary

The Cook family ran Parraweena Bird Sanctuary on their land and managed part of the adjoining reserve. The present park combines 4 hectares of Council reserve, 1.1 hectares of road easement and 0.7 hectares donated by the family.

Pair bushland with the coast

Find the reserve and pair it with the Eli Creek shoreline. For a picnic or playground, use the parks comparison.

What we still don’t know

The sanctuary’s full operating timeline and bird collection remain open questions. Old photographs and attributed memories may help fill those gaps.
