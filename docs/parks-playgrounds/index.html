ESA Park: play and a picnic

On The Esplanade between Aplin and Flinders streets, ESA Park has off-street parking, a playground, picnic shelters, barbecues, seating, toilets, showers and water. Accessible park facilities are described in the accessibility guide.

Shade sails cover swings and climbing equipment in the July 2024 photograph below. The park’s warm, shielded lights protect the adjacent turtle rookery.

The Gables: picnic and history

Bring a picnic for the shelter, seating and barbecue beside the foreshore. There is a water tap. Read the boarding-house story for the name behind the park.

Webb Park and quieter reserves

Webb Park on Murphy Street has a playground with a slide, basketball, seating and shelter. Find it on the map.

Choose Parraweena Park for bushland and sanctuary history, or Ian Drewe Memorial Park on Banksia Street for open grass.

Beside the water

These beaches are unpatrolled. Check the tide before walking onto the rock flats; keep a clear route back and stay clear of boat ramps.

Dog boundaries include playground exclusions.
