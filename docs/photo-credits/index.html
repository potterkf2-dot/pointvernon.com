Photographs around Point Vernon

Photographs credited to Point Vernon Guide were taken around the suburb. Photographers retain their copyright; contributed images carry the agreed credit.

The gallery includes the shoreline, Gatakers Bay, the shared path and park scenes.

Image editing and privacy

Images are cropped, resized and lightly adjusted; localised people removal in the foreshore and playground photographs reconstructs the areas they obscured, a vehicle registration is blurred in the walking photograph, and the kangaroo photograph has tonal adjustments only.

Permission and removal

For a credit change, photograph removal or privacy concern, send the page address and image details to hello@pointvernon.com.
