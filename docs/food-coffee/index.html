Directions to Vernon 5 Ways Takeaway

Picnic supplies

Bring a ready-to-eat picnic or buy supplies before heading to the coast. Choose the park facilities to suit your group, then collect what you need.

Fraser Coast Gourmet Meats is at Shop 4, 15–17 Preston Street. Use the business’s own page for products and current trading information.

Take it outside

Choose ESA Park or The Gables for a park picnic. Bring drinking water, take your rubbish away, and keep food away from wildlife. A ready-to-eat option leaves you free to picnic even when a barbecue is busy.

More choice in Hervey Bay

Pialba, Scarness and Torquay offer a wider choice of cafés, supermarkets and evening dining. Browse the regional visitor website and use each business’s own page for the menu, hours and bookings.
