Mangroves beside Eli Creek

Eli Creek beach lies between the creek mouth and Eli Point, beside Parraweena Park. The shore has high-tide sand, scattered mangroves and wide ridged tidal flats.

Start from the reserve

The neighbouring bushland reserve entrance is at Spence and Corser streets. Find the reserve on the map and keep to public access. View the creek and mangroves from firm ground; keep clear of soft sediment and creek channels.

For picnic or play facilities, choose a separate stop from the parks guide.

A changing creek edge

Lower water reveals more flats, while higher water brings the waterline closer to the mangroves. Explore the coastal habitats from the shore.

These beaches are unpatrolled. Check the tide before walking onto the rock flats; keep a clear route back and stay clear of boat ramps.
