Five Ways, Point Vernon

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Find Five Ways

5 Ways Convenience is on Corser Street in Point Vernon. If you have searched for “5 Ways Point Vernon”, “Five Ways shops” or “Vernon 5 Ways Takeaway”, start with the business at 52 Corser Street. Use the street address in your navigation app so you arrive at the shop rather than a general Point Vernon map pin.

Directions to 5 Ways Convenience. For the rest of your outing, the Point Vernon map and access guide shows the coastal parks, beaches and boat ramps.

Menu and opening hours

Use 5 Ways Convenience’s own page for its latest menu, contact details and trading announcements. Menu photographs and prices can change, so check the date before choosing an order. If a particular meal is the reason for your trip, confirm that it is available before setting off.

Check takeaway-kitchen serving times as well as store opening hours. A shop being open does not necessarily mean every cooked item is available throughout the day. Ask the business directly about public-holiday trading, larger orders and any food allergy or dietary requirement. Plan a separate stop if you need a guaranteed sit-down meal.

Arriving and parking

Follow parking signs and choose a legal space near your destination. Keep driveways and pedestrian routes clear while collecting an order. If you are towing a caravan or boat, choose a suitable stopping place before turning into a small parking area; a map pin does not confirm that there is room to manoeuvre a trailer.

If step-free access, space for a mobility aid or a seat while waiting will decide your visit, ask the business about the entrance and collection point in advance. The accessible visit guide helps with planning the foreshore part of the day.

Make it a picnic

Choose your picnic place before collecting food, especially when your group needs toilets or shelter. ESA Park combines a playground and foreshore stop. For a quieter picnic with a view, The Gables has an undercover shelter, seating, a barbecue and a water tap listed by Council. Compare the parks and playgrounds before you leave.

Bring drinking water and something ready to eat if you do not want to rely on a free barbecue. Carry food in a cool bag, take rubbish home if bins are full, and keep food away from wildlife. Check the tide and weather guide if you plan to walk on the shore after lunch; a picnic on the grass does not depend on exposed rock pools.

Other local food stops

For coffee on the Esplanade or takeaway around Murphy and Preston streets, use the Point Vernon food and coffee guide. It links directly to Brew & View Cafe, Point Vernon Takeaway and the Preston Street butcher. Check each business separately rather than assuming neighbouring shops share opening hours.

Keep the food stop and the coastal outing flexible: collect supplies when the shop is open, then choose the park that suits the weather and your group. For a longer stay, the first visit guide helps connect food, walks and beaches without trying to fit the whole peninsula into one stop.
