Free weekly community 5 km

Point Vernon Foreshore Reserve parkrun

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Walk, jog, run, volunteer or watch beside the Point Vernon foreshore every Saturday morning.

  • StartSaturday at 7:00am
  • Distance5 km out and back
  • CostFree; register once with parkrun

The essentials

When and where is Point Vernon parkrun?

Point Vernon Foreshore Reserve parkrun is a free 5 km event held every Saturday at 7:00am. The start and finish are opposite 138 The Esplanade, Point Vernon.

Road access is near the intersection of The Esplanade and Wattle Street. The event is for walkers and runners at any pace, as well as volunteers and spectators.

See the official event page

No race experience needed

What should a first-timer do?

Register with parkrun once before your first event and bring a scannable copy of your barcode if you want a recorded time.

  1. Register free with parkrun if this is your first parkrun. Your existing parkrun registration works here too.
  2. Save or bring a scannable copy of your barcode and review the official event page for updates.
  3. Arrive before the 7:00am start so you have time to find the meeting point, park or secure your bicycle, and hear the briefings.
  4. Walk, jog or run at your own pace, or ask the team about volunteering. Walking is welcome.

Along The Esplanade

What is the Point Vernon course like?

It is a 5 km out-and-back course entirely on a permanent paved path beside The Esplanade.

The route uses a public shared path, not a closed race course. Give way to other park users and watch for pedestrians, cyclists, children, dogs, wildlife, bollards and other obstacles. After rain, parts of the path may hold mud, leaves or puddles.

Read the official course description and map

Plan a low-stress arrival

Parking, transport and nearby facilities

Parking along The Esplanade is limited. The event asks people to walk or cycle where possible and to arrive early if driving.

The official course page says no public transport service currently enables arrival before the 7:00am start. Car-sharing can reduce pressure on the neighbourhood and other foreshore users.

Near the start

A shelter, bicycle rack and water bubbler are approximately 200 metres north of the start and finish.

Toilet

The official course page lists a unisex toilet approximately 200 metres south of the start and finish.

Water

Water bubblers are also listed along the course in both directions. Bringing your own water is still sensible in warm weather.

Afterwards

The event currently lists Brew and View Café for its weekly post-parkrun coffee. See the official page for changes.

Share the path

Children, dogs and buggies

  • Children under 11 must remain within arm’s reach of a parent, guardian or other appropriate adult throughout the event.
  • One dog may take part beside you on a short, handheld, non-extendable lead. Waist harnesses are not permitted.
  • Buggies are welcome, but participants should take care around others and start in a position appropriate to their pace.
  • Running with both a pushchair and a dog at the same time is not permitted.

See before travelling

Cancellations, volunteering and current updates

Outdoor events can be cancelled because of weather, path conditions or other local circumstances. See the official event page and Facebook page before making a special trip, especially after severe weather.

The event is organised by volunteers. Use the official volunteer page to find out how to help, or ask the team at the event which roles would suit you.
