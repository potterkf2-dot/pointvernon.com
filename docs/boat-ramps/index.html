The Gatakers Bay and The Gables Council entries provide these facility details. Council’s low-tide restriction listing does not guarantee depth or clearance for a particular vessel. Read the ramp signs and assess conditions before launching.

Plan launch and retrieval

The Gables is listed as low-tide restricted, but its Council entry does not specify a minimum tide height. Allow for the water level when you return as well as when you leave. Use tide and marine weather sources, read the ramp signs and assess the conditions for your vessel. A tide prediction alone does not establish clearance over submerged rock or the suitability of a ramp.

Ramp cleaning and access

Cleaning and inspections are arranged close to low tide where practicable. Council warns that ramps may close partly or fully during servicing and the schedule can change. Open Council’s current cleaning schedule before travelling and review MSQ’s Capricorn Coast maritime notices. For a Council facility enquiry, call 1300 79 49 29.

MSQ’s project list, updated in June 2026, includes dredging the Gatakers Point access channel. It gives no work dates, so check current notices for any access changes.

Water, toilets and shore-side waiting

Council lists no water or toilets at either ramp. Bring drinking water and choose a separate facilities stop using the parks guide. ESA Park has toilets including an all-abilities toilet; The Gables park has picnic facilities, while its Council record does not list toilets. Plan those stops before launching, and keep waiting groups and pedestrians clear of reversing trailers.

Once you leave the ramp

Use the marine-park maps and fishing rules for your destination. Marine Rescue Queensland Hervey Bay asks boaters to log on and off; its listed routine log-on channel is VHF 73. Follow the unit’s current instructions. In a marine emergency, use VHF channel 16 or call 000.
