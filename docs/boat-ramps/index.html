Point Vernon boat ramps

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Gatakers Bay has a two-lane ramp; The Gables has one lane and a low-tide restriction. Plan the launch and return for your vessel.

  • Gatakers BayTwo lanes; no low-tide restriction
  • The GablesOne lane; low-tide restricted
  • FacilitiesNo water or toilets at either ramp

Plan launch and retrieval

The Gables has a low-tide restriction. Allow for the water level when you return as well as when you leave. Use tide and marine weather sources and the signs at the ramp for your vessel.

Ramp cleaning and access

Cleaning and inspections are arranged close to low tide where practicable and can delay access. Open Council’s ramp schedule before a boating trip. For a facility enquiry, call 1300 79 49 29.

Water, toilets and shore-side waiting

Neither ramp has water or toilets. Bring drinking water and plan a separate facilities stop using the parks guide. Keep waiting groups and pedestrians clear of reversing trailers.

Once you leave the ramp

Use the marine-park maps and fishing rules for your destination. For trip logging, open Marine Rescue Queensland Hervey Bay. In a marine emergency, use VHF channel 16 or call 000.
