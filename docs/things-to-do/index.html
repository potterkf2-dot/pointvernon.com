Ten ideas for a first visit

  1. Look across Gatakers Bay

    Start beside the Corfield Street approach for the narrow beach and broad rock shelves. Keep the working ramp clear while enjoying the reserve.

  2. Play and picnic at ESA Park

    Make the park your base for a family outing. Add a short foreshore look after the picnic.

  3. Walk the 5 km foreshore course

    Start opposite 138 The Esplanade near Wattle Street. The paved out-and-back course returns to the same point.

  4. See the rocky shoreline at lower tide

    Lower water reveals the shelves and pools hidden at high tide. Use the tide predictions to choose the time for your visit.

  5. Picnic at The Gables

    Choose a foreshore park stop with a boarding-house story. Read about Dunn’s Enclosure before looking across the tidal remains.

  6. Explore Parraweena Park

    Enter the bushland reserve at Spence and Corser streets. Remnant eucalypt forest surrounds traces of the former bird sanctuary.

  7. Look at Eli Creek’s mangroves

    View the creek-edge habitat from firm public ground. Pair the stop with neighbouring Parraweena Park.

  8. Join Saturday parkrun

    Walk, jog, run or volunteer at the free weekly 5 km. The event starts at 7:00am opposite 138 The Esplanade.

  9. Bring binoculars to the foreshore

    Enjoy bay views from Gatakers Bay, ESA Park or The Gables. In whale season, boats offer a better chance of a close sighting.

  10. Follow the stories behind the names

    Read about The Gables, Charles Polson and the old bird sanctuary. The history guides lead to the surviving places and original sources.

Beside the shore

These beaches are unpatrolled. Check the tide before walking onto the rock flats; keep a clear route back and stay clear of boat ramps.

A short stop or a longer outing

A short stop

Choose Gatakers Bay for a view across the water and a short return stroll. Keep the working ramp clear.

About an hour

Make ESA Park your base for play, a picnic and a look at the shoreline. See the facilities before choosing your stop.

Half a day

Pair a park picnic with The Gables’ history, or add Parraweena Park. Allow for travel between stops and choose the pace that suits your group.

These are outing ideas, rather than measured route times. For a set distance, use the 5 km foreshore walk.
