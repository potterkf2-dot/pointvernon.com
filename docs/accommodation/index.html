Accommodation in and near Point Vernon

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Point Vernon is primarily residential, but two holiday parks provide local starting points. Hervey Bay offers a wider range of hotels, apartments and visitor accommodation.

Two local holiday parks

Point Vernon has two holiday parks for cabins and powered sites. Choose the location and space that suit your vehicle, then book directly with the operator.

Sunlodge Caravan Park

At 26 Mant Street, opposite Gatakers Bay, Sunlodge offers self-contained cabins and powered caravan sites with individual ensuites, a pool and barbecue facilities. Tents are not permitted on its slab sites.

Dogs need management consent. Extra vehicles, boats and leisure equipment require approval. Call 07 4128 1692 or open Sunlodge’s website.

BIG4 Hervey Bay Holiday Park

At 26 Corser Street, the park offers cabins, powered sites, a pool, playground and covered camp kitchen. Ask the operator about site size, pets and the room or entrance you need.

Call 07 4128 1423 or open the park’s booking page.

Choose your base

For boating, use the ramp comparison and arrange trailer parking with the park. For travel without a car, read the on-demand transport guide.

Pialba, Scarness, Torquay and Urangan provide wider accommodation choices. Use the regional visitor website to explore those areas.
