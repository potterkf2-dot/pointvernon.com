An offshore habitat installed in 2025

Point Vernon Artificial Reef has 88 concrete bombora structures, placed offshore in 2025. The modules have openings and shaded surfaces where marine organisms can settle.

It is about one nautical mile from Gatakers boat ramp, in water approximately 6–8 metres deep. Use Queensland Parks’ locality map for its position.

Artificial reef and natural shoreline

The offshore modules are separate from the natural reef and rock pools visible along the coast. Gatakers Bay and Gables Point also have designated no-anchoring areas; use the marine-park maps and rules for their boundaries.

Planning an offshore visit

Use current charts, marine weather and notices, and plan within your vessel’s limits. Compare launch points and arrange trip logging with Marine Rescue Queensland.
