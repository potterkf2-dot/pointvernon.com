A coastal residential suburb

Point Vernon occupies the north-western end of Hervey Bay’s urban coastline. The Esplanade follows the foreshore, while inland streets connect local homes with Pialba’s shops and services.

At the 2021 Census, Point Vernon had 5,718 residents, a median age of 52 and 2,616 private dwellings. Separate houses made up 93.4% of occupied private dwellings. These are Census figures, rather than current property-market estimates.

Plan your everyday journeys

Use on-demand transport for nominated shopping and health destinations, including Hervey Bay Hospital. Find parks and foreshore stops and save local service contacts.

Use EdMap for the exact address’s school catchment and StartingBlocks for approved childcare services. Enrolments and vacancies belong with the provider.

Research the exact property

Search Council mapping for planning, flood and storm-tide overlays. Flooding, coastal exposure and drainage vary by lot; read the layer’s legend and seek property-specific advice.

Work through the property checklist with your solicitor and suitable building or insurance professionals.

Before moving day

Arrange electricity and internet for the premises, test essential journeys at the times you will use them, and plan a nearby park or community activity for your first week. Ergon Retail’s moving information is a starting point for electricity.
