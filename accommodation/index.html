Accommodation in and near Point Vernon

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Point Vernon is primarily residential, but two holiday parks provide local starting points. Hervey Bay offers a wider range of hotels, apartments and visitor accommodation.

  • Local starting pointsTwo operator-listed holiday parks
  • For more choiceCompare the wider Hervey Bay area

Direct operator information

Compare two local holiday parks

Start with the type of accommodation and the space your vehicle needs. Both parks list cabins and powered sites, but their layouts and booking conditions differ.

26 Mant Street

Sunlodge Caravan Park

A small park opposite Gatakers Bay. Its operator lists self-contained water-view cabins and powered caravan sites with individual ensuites, plus a pool and barbecue facilities.

  • Bringing a tent? The booking policy says tents are not permitted because the sites have slabs. Ask directly about any other camping setup.
  • Bringing a dog? Pet-friendly options are advertised, but dogs require management consent and cannot be left unattended.
  • Bringing a boat or second vehicle? The standard allowance is one vehicle. Additional vehicles and leisure equipment need approval before arrival. Give the full length of your caravan, including its drawbar.

Call Sunlodge: 07 4128 1692 · See accommodation and the park map

Read rates, booking conditions and send an enquiry. The online form asks the park to confirm availability; submitting it is not a confirmed reservation.

26 Corser Street

BIG4 Hervey Bay Holiday Park

The operator lists self-contained ensuite cabins, powered sites, a saltwater pool, playground and covered camp kitchen with barbecues. It describes the water’s edge as approximately 200 metres away.

Use the park map to discuss where your cabin or site sits relative to amenities and recreation areas. Confirm the exact site dimensions, any pet conditions and the route to the foreshore before booking.

Call BIG4 Hervey Bay: 07 4128 1423 · Open the operator’s park map

View accommodation and booking options · Check park facilities

Operator sources checked on 19 September 2026. Confirm current availability and terms for your exact dates and accommodation. A nearby beach or ramp does not establish a step-free route from your site.

Look beyond the nightly price

What to confirm before booking

For a boat-based stay, compare Gatakers Bay and The Gables ramps as well as the park’s trailer arrangements. For a pet-friendly stay, check the separate dog rules on public beaches and paths; accommodation permission does not cover the foreshore.

Compare wider Hervey Bay accommodation

Pialba, Scarness, Torquay and Urangan provide a broader selection. Choose the area for the activities and transport you actually need rather than assuming every Hervey Bay stay is close to Point Vernon.

For a stay without a car, check Point Vernon’s on-demand transport rules and hours before booking. For mobility needs, ask the operator for details or photographs of the exact room, bathroom and entrance using the access checklist.
