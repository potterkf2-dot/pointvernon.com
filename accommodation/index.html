Direct operator information

Two Point Vernon starting points

Sunlodge Caravan Park

The operator lists a small beachfront park at 26 Mant Street with cabins, caravan sites and pet-friendly options. Confirm the exact accommodation type, accessibility, pet conditions and current rates directly.

Visit the Sunlodge website

BIG4 Hervey Bay Holiday Park

The operator lists cabins and powered sites at 26 Corser Street, with a pool, playground and camp kitchen and the shoreline approximately 200 metres away. Confirm current facilities, access and booking conditions directly.

Visit the BIG4 Hervey Bay website
