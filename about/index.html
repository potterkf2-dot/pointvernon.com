About Point Vernon Guide

Last updated: 12/08/2026

Why this site exists

A focused guide to one place

Point Vernon often appears as a short paragraph inside broader Hervey Bay guides. This site gives the headland enough room to explain its different shoreline sections, tidal character, paths, parks, history and practical cautions.

It is not an official tourism, Council or emergency-information service. The most current advice from public agencies, land managers and event organisers always takes priority.

How I check things

How information is checked

  • Prefer primary sources. Council, Queensland Government, Bureau of Meteorology, Translink, Beachsafe and official organiser pages are used wherever possible.
  • Separate fact from judgement. Descriptions aim to be specific rather than promotional.
  • Date-sensitive details need checking. Hours, timetables, events, facilities and rules can change after publication.
  • Safety language stays cautious. No web page can assess water, weather, access or personal capability for a particular visit.
  • Unconfirmed details are omitted or kept general. The guide does not turn an uncertain local observation into a precise public claim.

No pay-to-play

How businesses are listed

There are no paid rankings, affiliate links or sponsored placements. A local business may be included when it helps answer a practical visitor question. Inclusion is not a review, and omission is not a negative judgement.

Business owners are welcome to correct basic factual details, but cannot buy preferred wording or placement.

Local photographs

Photos, privacy and alt text

Photographs are taken locally or contributed by residents with permission. Contributors are credited, and anyone who wants a photograph of themselves or their property removed can ask.

Alternative text describes the useful content of each photograph for people who cannot see it.

Corrections welcome

Contact the guide

Corrections, photographs, event listings and complaints all go to hello@pointvernon.com. Every message gets read.

Include the page address and a reliable public source when suggesting a factual correction. Do not email private personal information unless it is necessary.

Corrections are assessed for evidence and relevance. Personal information, promotional copy and material that could expose a sensitive cultural or ecological location will not be published simply because it was submitted.
