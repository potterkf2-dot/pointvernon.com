A local guide to Point Vernon

This independent guide brings Point Vernon’s beaches, walks, parks and everyday details together in one place. Start with planning a visit, explore local life or find a guide.

The site owner is the publisher and makes the final decisions about content and corrections. This is not an official Council, tourism or emergency service.

How we check information

We use Council, government, transport and organiser sources wherever possible, and link to them so you can check the details. Local observations are identified as such; uncertain information is qualified or left out. Current official advice and on-site signs take priority.

Events are reviewed weekly, businesses and transport monthly, and facilities, dog rules and other public-service information at least every three months. Page dates record a review or substantive change, not a live status check. Follow the official links for today’s tides, weather, warnings, hours and access.

Business listings and independence

Businesses are included when they answer a useful local question and their details can be checked. Inclusion is not an endorsement; omission is not a negative judgement. We do not publish paid rankings, sponsored recommendations or “best” lists. Businesses cannot buy changes to facts, corrections or the order of ordinary listings.

A relevant personal, family or financial connection must be disclosed, or the item neutrally limited or omitted. Removal requests are assessed for accuracy, safety, privacy, copyright and public usefulness.

Advertising and sponsorship

There are currently no paid placements, affiliate links or sponsors. Before accepting money, we will update these standards. Any future paid placement must:

  • Be labelled “Advertisement”, “Sponsor” or “Affiliate link” beside it, with any payment from clicks or purchases explained.
  • Use a separate, fixed position without influencing editorial selection, factual wording or safety advice.
  • Avoid behavioural advertising, unnecessary tracking and conflicts involving emergency, safety, property or government information.

Sponsored material will not imitate independent advice. Sponsors will not receive private emails or Analytics data that identifies an individual.

Local photographs

See photo credits for attribution, significant editing and permission or removal requests. If a page or image is difficult to use, please let us know.

Get in touch

Send corrections, suggestions, photographs or complaints to hello@pointvernon.com. Include the page address and the detail that needs attention, plus a reliable source or the date and place of your observation.

We prioritise safety issues and materially misleading errors, check the evidence, and update the relevant guide and its date when a factual correction is needed. Disputed details may be qualified. Send only the personal information needed; private details and sensitive cultural or ecological locations will not be published simply because they are submitted. See our privacy information.
