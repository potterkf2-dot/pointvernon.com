# Content maintenance

Review pages regularly and after a reported change. Use each business or organiser's current information and public agency sources. Publish precise local access and facility details only when supported by a source or dated visit notes.

Keep one visible update date per page and matching Article/dateModified and sitemap dates. Remove expired notices. Avoid announcing short-term hours without a removal plan. Keep advertising disabled and GA4 opt-in unless the publisher changes that decision and updates the privacy explanation and hosting policy together.

Run the source validator and consent checks before release. Review every changed layout on a phone and desktop. Use the field checklist and photo shot list for missing details; retain photo permission and agreed credits.
