Public standards

Editorial, corrections and commercial policy

Effective: 22/08/2026

These rules protect the usefulness and independence of Point Vernon Guide. They apply to research, corrections, business mentions and any future commercial arrangement.

  • Editorial controlRetained by the responsible publisher
  • Paid placementNone at present
  • CorrectionsEvidence reviewed and dated

Editorial purpose

Useful local information comes first

The guide exists to help people make practical decisions about Point Vernon’s shoreline, parks, access, local services, history and property research. It does not publish a claim merely because it is popular, promotional or likely to attract search traffic.

The site owner is the responsible publisher and retains final control over inclusion, wording, prominence, corrections and removal.

Evidence and review

Sources, dates and uncertainty

  • Primary public sources are preferred for rules, facilities, transport, safety, planning and public services.
  • Organiser-owned sources are preferred for events and recurring activities.
  • Information that can change is given a check or update date and follows the site’s maintenance schedule.
  • On-ground observations are identified as observations; they do not override a current authority or on-site sign.
  • Unresolved conflicts or gaps are qualified, attributed or left out.

The linked authority, emergency service, land manager or current on-site sign takes priority if it conflicts with this guide.

Neutral selection

Listings, reviews and rankings

A business, event or organisation may be mentioned when it directly answers a local question and its basic details can be supported. Inclusion is not an endorsement, and omission is not a negative judgement.

The guide does not currently publish paid rankings, affiliate links, sponsored recommendations or “best” lists. A business cannot pay to alter factual wording, remove a fair correction, control organic ordering or prevent a relevant alternative from being included.

Evidence-led changes

Corrections and complaints

  1. Send the page address, the specific statement and a reliable public source or first-hand evidence.
  2. The responsible publisher checks the evidence, urgency and privacy implications.
  3. Safety-critical and materially misleading errors are prioritised. Minor wording or preference disputes may take longer.
  4. A material factual correction updates the visible review date and is recorded in verified updates.
  5. If evidence remains disputed, the page may explain the uncertainty rather than present one account as settled.

Requests containing threats, private accusations or unnecessary personal information will not be published. A correction does not guarantee the wording requested by the sender.

No hidden influence

Advertising, sponsorship and affiliate standards

There are no paid placements, affiliate links or sponsors at present. Before any commercial placement appears, Point Vernon Guide will:

  • label it clearly as “Advertisement”, “Sponsor” or “Affiliate link” beside the placement;
  • keep payment separate from editorial selection, factual wording and safety guidance;
  • avoid behavioural advertising and unnecessary tracking;
  • state whether a click or purchase may generate payment;
  • use fixed commercial positions rather than selling organic rank;
  • exclude arrangements that create a conflict around emergency, safety, property or government information; and
  • update this policy before accepting money.

Sponsored editorial written to imitate independent advice will not be used. Any future sponsor must not receive access to private correction emails or Analytics data that identifies an individual.

Accountability

Conflicts, removals and privacy

A material personal, family or financial connection relevant to a recommendation must be disclosed or the item must be reassigned, neutrally limited or omitted. Photograph permissions and contributor contact details are managed separately from public credits.

Removal requests are assessed for accuracy, safety, privacy, copyright and public usefulness. A factual public record is not automatically removed because it is inconvenient, and private personal details are not added merely because they were submitted.

Contact

Send a correction or policy question

Email hello@pointvernon.com. Include only the information needed to assess the request.
