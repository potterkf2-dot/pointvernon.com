Exact-place shoreline guides

Compare Point Vernon’s four beach records

Beachsafe lists four records in Point Vernon. They face different directions and have different mixes of sand, tidal rock flats, creek habitat and foreshore reserve.

Eli Creek

A short north-facing creek-edge beach with mangroves and extensive tidal flats.

Gatakers Bay

An east-facing bay with a narrow high-tide beach, broad rock flats and a working boat ramp.

Point Vernon beach

The 1.5-kilometre east-facing record along the northern shoreline, backed by foreshore reserve and The Esplanade.

Gables Point

The long south-facing foreshore with a narrow high-tide beach and broad rock flats.
