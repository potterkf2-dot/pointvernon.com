Why visit Point Vernon

A different side of the Hervey Bay foreshore

Point Vernon is a coastal suburb where sandy stretches meet rock platforms and low-energy bays. It suits an unhurried visit: look at the tide, choose a part of the shoreline and leave time to stop.

A useful first stop

Start with Gatakers Bay

Gatakers Bay brings several parts of Point Vernon together in one place: a beach, exposed rock flats at lower tides, foreshore parkland, fishing and a public boat ramp.

It is not a patrolled swimming beach. Check the tide and conditions, supervise children around the water and take care on wet or uneven rocks.

Before you go

  • Check the tide if you want to explore the shoreline.
  • Use current marine and fishing rules, not an old summary.
  • Keep clear of boats and working ramp areas.
