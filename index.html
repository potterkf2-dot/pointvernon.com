Point Vernon · Hervey Bay · Queensland

Point Vernon, a coastal suburb of Hervey Bay

Last updated: 16/08/2026

Four separately catalogued beach sections wrap around the suburb: Eli Creek, Gatakers Bay, Point Vernon beach and Gables Point.

Each part of the coast faces a different direction and has its own mix of sand, tidal rock flats, creek-edge habitat and foreshore reserve. This independent guide helps you choose where to start and what to check before you go.

Why visit Point Vernon

A different side of the Hervey Bay foreshore

Point Vernon is a coastal suburb where sandy stretches meet rock platforms and low-energy bays. It suits an unhurried visit: look at the tide, choose a part of the shoreline and leave time to stop.

Not just Gatakers Bay

Choose the part of the coast that suits you

Beachsafe catalogues four separate beach sections around Point Vernon. Gatakers Bay is only one of them, and the names do not always match the nearby park or street name a local might use.

The four beach records

  • Eli Creek: a short north-facing creek-edge beach.
  • Gatakers Bay: a sheltered curve with a working boat ramp.
  • Point Vernon beach: the east-facing northern shoreline.
  • Gables Point: the long south-facing foreshore.
