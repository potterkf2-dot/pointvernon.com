Checked 11 September 2026 at 2 pm AEST

This weekend: 12–13 September

  • Point Vernon parkrun: the organiser advertises a free 5 km event at Point Vernon Foreshore Reserve at 7 am Saturday. Point Vernon was not on the official cancellation list when checked, but late cancellations remain possible, so check again before travelling. Read the event details and registration requirements.
  • Cards at Hervey Bay Library: Council’s program lists a free, no-booking card-playing session from 9 am to 3 pm Saturday; children under 12 need a guardian. Check the current Council events calendar.
  • Urangan tide highlights: the official table predicts a 3.53 m high at 9:15 am and a 0.59 m low at 3:27 pm Saturday; Sunday’s predicted high is 3.49 m at 9:51 am and low is 0.76 m at 4:04 pm. Times are AEST. These are Urangan predictions, not Point Vernon observations or an assurance of access or safety. Check the Maritime Safety Queensland table.

Tide source: Maritime Safety Queensland’s 2026 Queensland Tide Tables; predictions © Commonwealth of Australia 2025, Bureau of Meteorology. Predictions only; actual water levels can vary with weather. Weekend details are retained here as a dated archive.

8 September 2026

Current rules and planning links clarified

  • Dogs on the foreshore: the guide now reflects Council’s July update, which expects Stage 2 beach changes later in 2026 or early 2027. Check the current dog-area guidance.
  • Fishing zones: the guide makes clear that the exact mapped zone determines the marine-park rules, including where the two-line restriction applies. Check marine zoning and fishing rules.
  • E-bikes and e-scooters: the local-help guide now describes the 31 August changes as in force and links directly to device-specific rules and exemptions. Find the current riding rules.
  • Buying a property: the property guide explains important information excluded from the mandatory seller disclosure statement. See the checks to make before buying.
  • Planning a visit: a working regional accessibility guide replaces a retired PDF, and the transport guide now links directly to the official booking portal. Plan access · Plan an on-demand journey.

8 September 2026

A mapped 5 km foreshore walking option

The walks guide now explains the 5 km out-and-back route documented by Point Vernon Foreshore Reserve parkrun, with its starting point and official course map. It also links directly to the tide, dog-area and accessibility guides to help plan the outing.

Plan a 5 km foreshore walk · Read the organiser’s course description

1 September 2026

Library family-program link and monthly checks refreshed

The local-help guide now points families to the official current library program calendar rather than copying changeable session dates. The monthly review also reconfirmed Point Vernon’s Translink on-demand details and removed three obsolete Council download links while retaining the working authority pages.

Find current library and Council details · Check Point Vernon transport

31 August 2026

Community Hub details updated and expired events archived

The local-help guide now records the Community Hub’s current library and Council service hours. The what’s-on guide has returned to its evergreen organiser-led format after the 29–30 August listings ended.

Check current Council service details · Find current event sources

29 August 2026

Whale Festival weekend listing added

The what’s-on guide linked to the organiser’s City and Sea, Parade of Lights and Paddle Out for Whales program for 29–30 August, with a reminder to confirm locations and weather arrangements before travelling. The expired listing was removed on 31 August.

Find current event sources

22 August 2026

Private-property caravan stays clarified

The local-help guide now separates private-property caravan questions from illegal camping in parks, roads and foreshores. It explains Council’s limited family-or-friends pathway and directs other arrangements to Council and tenancy guidance rather than presenting a suburb-wide answer.

Read local help and reporting contacts

21 August 2026

Household battery disposal guidance added

The local-help guide now warns against placing loose household batteries in kerbside bins and points to current Council disposal information. Damaged, hot, swollen or smoking batteries are treated as a fire-safety issue.

Read the battery and Council-service guidance

Recurring family literacy listing added

The what’s-on guide now includes a source-led recurring literacy activity and tells readers to verify the current date, age suitability and any booking requirement with the organiser before travelling.

How this update record works

This page records changes that could affect a practical decision or materially improve factual accuracy. Routine punctuation, layout and cache-busting edits are not listed.

Each linked guide remains the complete source of context. If an update conflicts with a public authority, organiser or current on-site sign, follow the responsible source and tell the guide.
