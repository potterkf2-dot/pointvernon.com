22 August 2026

Private-property caravan stays clarified

The local-help guide now separates private-property caravan questions from illegal camping in parks, roads and foreshores. It explains Council’s limited family-or-friends pathway and directs other arrangements to Council and tenancy guidance rather than presenting a suburb-wide answer.

Read local help and reporting contacts

21 August 2026

Household battery disposal guidance added

The local-help guide now warns against placing loose household batteries in kerbside bins and points to current Council disposal information. Damaged, hot, swollen or smoking batteries are treated as a fire-safety issue.

Read the battery and Council-service guidance

20 August 2026

Recurring family literacy listing added

The what’s-on guide now includes a source-led recurring literacy activity and tells readers to verify the current date, age suitability and any booking requirement with the organiser before travelling.

Check what’s on and current organiser sources

19 August 2026

Barking-dog reporting pathway added

The local-help guide now explains the non-urgent Council pathway for recurring barking concerns, while keeping immediate danger and animal-welfare emergencies with the appropriate service.

Find the appropriate reporting service

18 August 2026

Community Hub service notice added

The local-help guide now records the temporary Hervey Bay Library and Council customer-service move, with current dates and a direct Council source. Readers are asked to check the transition details before travelling.

See local service contacts

17 August 2026

Noticeboard claims routed to verifiable sources

The food, local-life and moving guides were updated after a community-noticeboard review. Time-sensitive claims are either linked to a responsible source, qualified or excluded when they cannot be reliably verified.

How this update record works

This page records changes that could affect a practical decision or materially improve factual accuracy. Routine punctuation, layout and cache-busting edits are not listed.

Each linked guide remains the complete source of context. If an update conflicts with a public authority, organiser or current on-site sign, follow the responsible source and tell the guide.
