Every Saturday

Point Vernon Foreshore Reserve parkrun

Point Vernon’s free weekly 5 km parkrun starts at 7:00am every Saturday from the foreshore reserve opposite 138 The Esplanade.

You can walk, jog, run, volunteer or spectate. Register with parkrun once and bring a scannable barcode if you want a recorded time. Check the official event channels before travelling in case the event is cancelled.

Browse locally

Markets around Hervey Bay

Market schedules can change for public holidays, weather, venue works and organiser decisions. Markets at the Pier, linked above, has its own current schedule; for other markets, start with the regional calendars and follow the organiser’s listing.

Before leaving for a market

  • Confirm the next date and start and finish times on the organiser’s page.
  • Check the venue, parking, access and any pet rules.
  • Check for a cancellation notice if the weather is uncertain.

For a day without a scheduled event, try the things-to-do guide for parks, walks and foreshore stops.

The bay through the year

Seasonal whale activity

Humpback whales are most commonly associated with Hervey Bay from July to October, with Queensland Government material describing August to October as the main whale-watching period in the bay. Tours, talks and related events can cluster around the season, but dates vary each year.

Use the Point Vernon whale-watching guide for cautious land-based tips, and book only through an operator whose current conditions, accessibility and cancellation policy suit you.

Smaller happenings

Community, family and neighbourhood listings

Small gatherings may be promoted through libraries, community organisations, schools, sporting groups or venue noticeboards. These can be useful, but the organiser remains the source of truth. Check whether an event is public, whether booking is required and whether photography or dogs are permitted.

Family activity · third Thursday

Rattle and Rhyme at Bunnings Hervey Bay

Fraser Coast Libraries’ current schedule lists a free 30-minute session for children under two and their parents or carers at 10:30am on the third Thursday of each month. It is held near the playground and café at Bunnings Hervey Bay, outside Point Vernon, and no booking is required.

Dates can change. Check the official event listing before travelling or browse Fraser Coast Libraries’ First 5 Forever activities.

The parkrun and Rattle and Rhyme organiser listings were rechecked on 8 September 2026. This page is a guide to recurring activities and current calendars; it does not show live cancellations.

Keep this guide useful

Suggest an event or correction

To suggest a public, relevant event, email the organiser’s name, official link, venue and accessibility information to hello@pointvernon.com. Listing an event is free, and there is no way to pay to appear here.

Never send private attendee lists or personal information that is not already meant for publication.
