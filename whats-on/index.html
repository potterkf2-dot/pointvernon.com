Browse locally

Markets around Hervey Bay

Market schedules can change for public holidays, weather, venue works and organiser decisions. Use the event’s own page for the date, operating hours, accessibility, parking and pet rules. A social-media post may be more current than a directory, but check when it was published.

Do not rely on a remembered routine

Even a long-running market can skip a date or move. Confirm on the morning if the weather is uncertain.

The bay through the year

Seasonal whale activity

Humpback whales are most commonly associated with Hervey Bay from July to October, with Queensland Government material describing August to October as the main whale-watching period in the bay. Tours, talks and related events can cluster around the season, but dates vary each year.

Use the Point Vernon whale-watching guide for cautious land-based tips, and book only through an operator whose current conditions, accessibility and cancellation policy suit you.

Smaller happenings

Community and neighbourhood listings

Small gatherings may be promoted through libraries, community organisations, schools, sporting groups or venue noticeboards. These can be useful, but the organiser remains the source of truth. Check whether an event is public, whether booking is required and whether photography or dogs are permitted.

Only current organiser details are published

This guide does not copy unconfirmed recurring days or times. Follow the official calendar or organiser link for the current date and venue.

Keep this guide useful

Suggest an event or correction

To suggest a public, relevant event, open a website correction request on GitHub with the organiser’s name, official link, venue and accessibility information. Inclusion is editorial and free; paid placement is not accepted.

Never send private attendee lists or personal information that is not already meant for publication.
