Public transport guide

Getting around Point Vernon without a car

Last checked: 16/08/2026

Translink’s Hervey Bay on-demand service covers all of Point Vernon, but it must be booked. It is not a fixed-route bus that you can simply wait for at any time.

  • CoverageAll of Point Vernon
  • Hours7:30am–5:30pm Monday–Saturday
  • BookingEssential, including the return trip

A roam-zone service

How the on-demand service works

You book a seat and are collected within the roam zone rather than following a conventional timetable along one fixed route. Translink says the roam zone includes all of Point Vernon and parts of Pialba.

You can travel from anywhere in the roam zone to a nominated key location, from a key location back into the zone, or between two key locations. A trip between arbitrary points outside those patterns may not be available.

Eight key locations

Where can it take you?

Shopping

Stockland Shopping Centre, Bay Plaza, Pialba Place and Fraser Shores.

Health

Hervey Bay Hospital is one of the nominated key locations.

Community

Hervey Bay RSL and Melory Place.

Learning

The University and Library key location.

Use Translink’s current map to confirm the exact pickup rules and key-location stops before relying on a journey.

Reserve before you travel

Three ways to book

  1. Use the Translink On Demand app.
  2. Use Translink’s online booking portal.
  3. Call Translink on 13 12 30.

Trips can be booked up to four weeks ahead, including recurring bookings. Translink says a booking is essential for both the outward and return journey.

Allow a collection window

Hours, arrival time and fare

The service currently operates from 7:30am to 5:30pm, Monday to Saturday, and does not run on Sundays or public holidays.

When you book, Translink provides a 30-minute arrival window and says it will usually send a notification about five minutes before the driver arrives. Actual waits depend on demand and other booked passengers.

Translink currently lists a flat 50-cent fare per journey, paid in cash to the driver. Recheck fares before travel because ticketing policy can change.

A common source of confusion

Route 765 is a school service

Do not plan a general trip around route 765

Although route 765 lists Point Vernon streets, Translink marks it as a school service only and directs passengers to Wide Bay Transit for information. It is not the suburb’s general all-day bus service.

Book for your needs

Accessible and assisted travel

Explain mobility equipment or boarding needs when booking and confirm whether the vehicle offered suits you. Allow extra time for appointments, book the return trip and keep the service contact available in case pickup details change.

For urgent medical care, do not rely on an on-demand public transport booking. Use the appropriate emergency or health service.
