Public transport guide

Getting around Point Vernon without a car

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Book Translink’s on-demand service for a journey between Point Vernon and its nominated Hervey Bay stops. Start with the destination rules, reserve the return trip and allow for the pickup window.

  • CoverageAll of Point Vernon
  • Hours7:30am–5:30pm Monday–Saturday
  • BookingEssential, including the return trip

A roam-zone service

How the on-demand service works

You book a seat and are collected within the roam zone rather than following a conventional timetable along one fixed route. Translink says the roam zone includes all of Point Vernon and parts of Pialba.

You can travel from anywhere in the roam zone to a nominated key location, from a key location back into the zone, or between two key locations. For example, a trip from a Point Vernon address to Hervey Bay Hospital fits the published pattern. A trip from one Point Vernon park to another is not one of the listed journey types; confirm your plan before relying on it.

Eight key locations

Where can it take you?

Shopping

Stockland Shopping Centre, Bay Plaza, Pialba Place and Fraser Shores.

Health

Hervey Bay Hospital is one of the nominated key locations.

Community

Hervey Bay RSL and Melory Place.

Learning

Translink still labels a key location “University and Library”. Confirm the actual stop when booking; the library is now at a different address.

Use Translink’s current map to confirm the exact pickup rules and key-location stops before relying on a journey.

Travelling to the new library or Council counter

Hervey Bay Library and Council’s customer-service counter are now at 23 Main Street, Pialba. Council says the former library on Old Maryborough Road has closed. Translink’s on-demand page still uses the “University and Library” destination label.

Give the booking service the new Main Street address and ask which confirmed stop serves your visit. Do not assume the old destination name means a direct drop-off at the new Hub. See Council’s current Hub location and access details and the local service guide.

Reserve before you travel

Book the outward and return journeys

  1. Choose the places and time. Have the pickup address, nominated destination, passenger numbers and any boarding needs ready.
  2. Reserve both journeys. Use the Translink booking options, the online booking portal or call 13 12 30.
  3. Keep the confirmation handy. Check the pickup place and arrival window before leaving, and use the booking service if your plans change.

Translink also offers the On Demand app. It lists advance bookings of up to four weeks, including recurring trips.

Allow a collection window

Hours, arrival time and fare

The service currently operates from 7:30am to 5:30pm, Monday to Saturday, and does not run on Sundays or public holidays.

When you book, Translink provides a 30-minute arrival window and says it will usually send a notification about five minutes before the driver arrives. Actual waits depend on demand and other booked passengers. For an appointment or onward connection, allow time for the collection window as well as the journey.

Translink currently lists a flat 50-cent fare per journey, paid in cash to the driver. Have cash ready and recheck the current fare on the official service page before travel.

A common source of confusion

Route 765 is a school service

Do not plan a general trip around route 765

Although route 765 lists Point Vernon streets, Translink marks it as a school service only and directs passengers to Wide Bay Transit for information. It is not the suburb’s general all-day bus service.

Book for your needs

Accessible and assisted travel

Explain mobility equipment or boarding needs when booking and confirm whether the vehicle offered suits you. Allow extra time for appointments, book the return trip and keep the service contact available in case pickup details change.

For urgent medical care, do not rely on an on-demand public transport booking. Use the appropriate emergency or health service.
